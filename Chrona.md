# Chrona: Time & Task Tracking in the Terminal

## What is Chrona?

Chrona is a terminal-based application for tracking both your tasks and your time, designed for developers and command-line enthusiasts who want to manage their productivity without leaving the terminal. 

While most productivity tools aim to be comprehensive suites with flashy interfaces, Chrona embraces terminal simplicity. It's built for people who live in the command line and want their productivity tools to live there too.

## Core Philosophy

Many of us who work in terminals struggle with the context-switching that comes from jumping to browser-based task managers or time trackers. Chrona solves this by keeping your focus where your work is—in the terminal.

At its heart, Chrona connects three essential elements:
- **Tasks** you're working on
- **Time** you're spending
- **Activity logs** of what you've done

These connections create a simple but powerful system for understanding your productivity patterns over time.

## Key Features

- **Task Management**: Create, organize, and track the status of tasks from Backlog to Done
- **Time Tracking**: Track time spent on specific tasks or general activities
- **Activity Logging**: Maintain a chronological record of your work 
- **Terminal-Native**: Fully keyboard-driven interface with intuitive navigation
- **Local-First**: All data stored in readable TOML files you control
- **Distraction-Free**: Focused interface that helps maintain flow states

## Who Should Use Chrona?

Chrona is ideal for:

- Developers who prefer keyboard-driven interfaces
- Terminal enthusiasts who dislike switching contexts
- Anyone who wants to understand their time usage patterns
- People who value privacy and local storage of productivity data
- Users who find modern productivity tools bloated and distracting

## Current State & Honest Assessment

The current version of Chrona provides basic task management capabilities. Tasks can be created, edited, and deleted, with four states (Backlog, Todo, In Progress, Done) and a split-screen view for comparing task lists.

While Chrona has a promising foundation, it's still evolving. Time tracking and activity logging features are planned but not yet implemented. The UI is functional but will become more refined and customizable with widgets that can be arranged into focused workflow views.

## Why Choose Chrona Over Alternatives?

While tools like Todoist, Jira, or Toggl offer more features, Chrona offers:

1. **Terminal-Native Experience**: No more context switching out of your workflow
2. **Simplicity**: Focused on what matters without feature bloat
3. **Transparency**: Plain text TOML files that you can inspect, back up, or version control
4. **Integrated Approach**: Tasks, time, and logs working together rather than as separate tools
5. **Privacy**: All your data stays on your machine

## Limitations (Currently)

In the spirit of transparency:

- Chrona doesn't currently support team collaboration features
- Mobile access is not available (and may not be a priority)
- The UI, while functional, is still being refined
- Time tracking and activity logging features are still in development

## The Road Ahead

Chrona is building toward a cohesive system where selecting a task automatically prepares it for time tracking, starting a session creates automatic log entries, and task completion updates your time reports.

Future releases will focus on implementing the time tracking core, activity logging, visual timeline views, and better analysis of productivity patterns.

---

Chrona is for people who value simplicity, focus, and the terminal. If that's you, Chrona might be the productivity tool you've been looking for.
